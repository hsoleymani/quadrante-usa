About Right Analytics

Founded in 2019, Right Analytics is a boutique power systems consultancy comprised of a small, highly knowledgeable team of engineers and analysts with a mix of academic and industrial experience.

The firm specializes in transmission planning, interconnection studies, large load and data center siting, and a broad range of advanced power systems analyses.
