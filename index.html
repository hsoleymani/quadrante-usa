About Right Analytics

Founded in 2019, Right Analytics is a boutique power systems consultancy comprised of a small, highly knowledgeable team of engineers and analysts with a mix of academic and industrial experience.

The firm specializes in transmission planning, interconnection studies, large load and data center siting, and a broad range of advanced power systems analyses.

About Quadrante

In a world where progress often clashes with nature, we embrace our purpose by always leading engineering and architecture projects with its Total Design approach, seeking economically optimised, sustainable solutions of technical excellence. As a multidisciplinary consultancy and design group in Engineering, Architecture, Environment and Sustainability, we take a holistic view of the construction sector and pursue an integrated and evolving approach to sustainability.

With a strong commitment to the planet and future generations, we are involved in several decarbonisation projects, making a significant contribution to mitigating the effects of climate change. By implementing innovative and sustainable solutions, we promote the reduction of carbon emissions in various sectors, including energy, transport and construction.

Our guiding values are based on the future, professionalism, ethics and flexibility. Through these values, we endeavour to attract and nurture talent, giving them unique opportunities for development.
