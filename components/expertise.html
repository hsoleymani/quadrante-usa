Hardware-in-the-Loop Testing

Testing

HIL testing allows you to validate your controller designs and other Intelligent Electronic Devices (IEDs) without requiring the full system hardware. Instead, a real-time plant simulator acts as a digital twin of the actual system—or portions of it—providing practical, cost-effective testing. We offer a comprehensive range of HIL tests to verify the performance of power electronics controllers, power system protection systems, and battery management systems.
