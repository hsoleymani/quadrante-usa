Quadrante USA LLC represents a transformative merger between Quadrante Global's multidisciplinary engineering excellence and Right Analytics' specialized US power systems expertise. This strategic combination creates a unified force driving grid decarbonization and sustainable infrastructure development across 20+ countries.

Founded on Quadrante's Total Design philosophy and Right Analytics' academic rigor, we deliver economically optimized, sustainable solutions that combine global scale with local market expertise. Since Right Analytics' establishment in 2019, our US operations have specialized in transmission planning, interconnection studies, data center siting, and advanced power systems analyses across major ISO markets.

Our merged capabilities span the complete infrastructure lifecycle—from Quadrante's expertise in energy transition, cities & territories, and mobility infrastructure to Right Analytics' deep knowledge of NYISO, PJM, MISO, and ERCOT markets. This combination ensures optimal outcomes for utilities, ISOs, developers, and major technology companies.

We utilize cutting-edge simulation tools, Hardware-in-the-Loop testing, and data-driven modeling while maintaining the agility and flexibility that defines our boutique approach. Our guiding values—future-focused vision, technical excellence, ethics, and adaptability—attract top talent globally while delivering locally relevant solutions.
