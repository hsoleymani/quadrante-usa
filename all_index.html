About Quadrante USA

Quadrante USA LLC is the unified engineering and consulting firm, strategically positioned to drive grid decarbonization and sustainable infrastructure globally. Leveraging decades of global scale and highly specialized technical expertise, we serve clients across the full construction life cycle in 20+ countries, including the United States, Europe, Latin America, and Africa.

In a world where progress often clashes with nature, we embrace our purpose by always leading engineering and architecture projects with our Total Design approach, seeking economically optimised, sustainable solutions of technical excellence. As a multidisciplinary consultancy, we take a holistic view of the construction sector, actively promoting the reduction of carbon emissions in energy, transport, and construction.

Our technical foundation, established by Right Analytics in 2019, specializes in transmission planning, interconnection studies, large load and data center siting, and advanced power systems analyses. This core expertise allows us to provide a decisive technical advantage by combining academic rigor with field-proven industry experience.

Our teams utilize cutting-edge simulation tools, regulatory insight, and data-driven modelling to deliver solutions and ensure optimal outcomes for utilities, ISOs, and developers. Our guiding values are based on the future, professionalism, ethics, and flexibility, ensuring we attract and nurture talent across the globe.
